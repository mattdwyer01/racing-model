# Betting test (backtest at SP, 2023 to 2026 test years, VIC/SA/QLD)

- Bet when p x SP - 1 > edge threshold; p = out-of-sample blended probability. Flat = 1 unit per bet; 1/4 Kelly = 25 x edge / (SP - 1) units per bet (100-unit notional bank, no compounding)
- Control: the same rule with p = calibrated SP only. ROI minus control = what the model adds
- Races dropped for an implausible SP book (outside 105% to 250%): 334 (missing runners or bad SPs; they create fake overlays)
- Not executable as is: SP is known only at the jump and the blend uses it. Needs the TAB fixed price at bet time to confirm
